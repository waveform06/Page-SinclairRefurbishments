About Us

Friendly, reliable service & high-quality work

Sinclair Refurbishments & Decorating is run by Charlie Sinclair, a sole trader based in Sutton, Surrey. From a single room refresh to a full refurbishment, every job gets the same careful preparation, clean working and attention to detail.

We work for both domestic and commercial customers, offer no-obligation appointments and quotes for jobs of any size, and have access to a trusted team of tradesmen when a project needs more than paint — including plastering, carpentry, tiling, brickwork and electrics.

What We Do

Our Services

Everything from a fresh coat of paint to a complete refurbishment, finished to a high standard.

INTERIOR DECORATING

Walls, ceilings, woodwork, stairs and landings — brightening rooms with clean lines and durable finishes.

EXTERIOR PAINTING

Render, windows, porches and guttering, with jet washing and thorough preparation before any paint goes on.

SPRAY PAINTING

Professional spray application for a smoother, factory-quality finish on walls, doors and joinery.

WALLPAPERING

Quality wall coverings, feature walls and murals hung neatly with perfectly matched patterns.

WINDOWS & TIMBER

Wooden windows and doors stripped and repainted, timber resin repairs and full timber restoration.

REFURBISHMENTS

Complete room, home and office refurbishments — including kitchen replacements — coordinated from start to finish.

PLASTERING & REPAIRS

Crack filling, making good and plastering so walls are smooth and ready for a flawless finish.

SPECIALIST FINISHES

Specialist paint effects, anti-vandal coatings, coving and bespoke finishes for distinctive spaces.
